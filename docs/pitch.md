# Turbo Town — pitch

Sam's game, from two interviews on 2026-09-26 (first ten answers in
`docs/interview-1.md`). Every decision below is Sam's.

## In one line
A 3D race through a city: pick your kid hero and your car's colour, race
five computer cars over three laps, fly off ramps and throw silly things.

## The decisions
| Topic | Sam's call |
|-------|-----------|
| Name | **Turbo Town** |
| Kind of game | Race, 3D, in a city |
| You are | A kid hero, picked at the start of each race |
| Your car | A normal car; you choose its colour |
| Crazy cars | Won by winning races (e.g. a hot dog car) |
| Racers | 6 cars: you and 5 computer racers |
| Laps | 3 |
| One race | About 3 minutes |
| On the track | Ramps and jumps |
| Throwables | Water balloons (splash, slows), jelly (stuck), banana skins (slip and spin). More later. |
| Controls | Game pad |

## The core loop (what has to be fun first)
Drive fast, fly off a ramp, throw something silly at the car ahead, cross
the line first. Everything else (hero picking, car colours, unlocking crazy
cars) comes after driving feels good.

## Open questions
- For Gareth: which game pad will Sam use? (Needed before controls work.)
- For Sam, later: which button throws, what the hero looks like, which
  crazy cars to win, what the city looks like.
